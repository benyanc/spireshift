copies learn original plans; teacher copies learn the selected plans. Each copy
gets 16 epochs × 4 updates × 32 examples: **64 updates / 2,048 draws**. Four copies
therefore receive 256 updates. Worlds are weighted equally before drawing a root
and position; longer trajectories or multiple roots do not get extra world weight.

selection. Navigation and RNG visits commit only after actual acknowledged delivery.
All legal choices remain present. The routed arm is an experiment control, not a
permanent restriction on future learned policies. Its direct contrast with teacher

After the new-policy canaries, up to 32 withheld-fight checks use exact original
openings followed by each evaluated player's own choices; no teacher choice is
forced after the root. Then 16 new full-game worlds × 2 fixed initializers × 4 modes

original denominators. More than two technical failures stops the finite evaluation.
Paired uncertainty clusters both fixed initializers by world. All-zero observed
wins do not prove equal population win probabilities. No result promotes a player
automatically or qualifies an adaptive controller.
