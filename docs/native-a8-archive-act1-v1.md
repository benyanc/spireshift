# Qualify more normal-start A8 Act 1 demonstrations

The [48-game comparison](native-a8-archive-copy-fit-v1.md) found zero A8 wins
and zero Act 1 clears for both originals and both copies fitted on three
full-run demonstrations. The fitted players reached a higher floor in six
of 24 pairs. Most games ended in ordinary fights or elites before the boss.
The next training-data priority is broader early-game success across worlds.

The Spire Codex catalog returned 33 solo, standard Ironclad/A8 winning
journals for v0.111.0. Eight had prior screening or native attempts. None
of the other 25 passed full-run source admission. Nine have a continuous
first segment through the recorded Act 2 entry with the exact build,
unmodified start, one successful attempt per fight and the Act 1 boss
exit. They span five Act 1 bosses. The source screen is sealed as
`sha256:4aacc4fdaa9ffc02e00cac416ff66f3c82cd4fc585082f7b811d7f8a136c4067`.
[Spire Codex replay API](https://github.com/ptrlrd/spire-codex/blob/main/contributing/API_REFERENCE.md#replays).

The qualifier consumes only the first segment through the recorded Act 2
entry. It regenerates the native world at Neow and binds each recorded
choice to its current public owner and complete legal menu. The last
delivery must be Proceed from the native Act 1 boss rewards, with a living
player at the declared Act 2 map. It reconstructs raw observations, choice
bindings, receipts and successors before exporting any labels. Ordered
selection factors stay with the committed answer. The complete later
journey and all full-run victory evidence remain separate.

The first control preserved one floor-six divergence after 37 verified
deliveries. The source recorded the enemies before a first-turn relic killed
one, while native exposed the settled playable turn. The other opening had
a different Neow offer set. It sent zero actions and shut down cleanly.
The first audit expected an action receipt file in that case and stopped.
The v2 saved check reconstructs the no-action observation and verifies the
empty receipt and action streams. Both worlds retain their original attempt
and supply zero labels. The continuation reserves their places in the
nine-source denominator.

Six sources belong to fitting and three to a separate demonstration
holdout. The candidate threshold is at least four verified fitting clears
spanning three Act 1 bosses and two verified holdout clears, with every
attempt resolved. A clean source divergence remains in the denominator.
The qualifier freezes the game, runtime, adapter and full model and Adam
states. It makes zero optimizer updates.

All nine attempts resolved with two verified native Act 2 entries and
seven clean divergences. The two clears contributed 484 delivered answers.
The training-designated source beat Ceremonial Beast and entered Act 2 at
49 HP. The held-out source beat The Kin and entered at 51 HP. The bank
missed the declared fitting threshold. Preserve both clears and their
partitions, and keep the seven stops outside training. The qualification
report is `sha256:4b5d1966fed9e74f3c74dad6bd8abfcdecfcce9acba8c8e6a024532f49aed6c1`.

The saved journals distinguish the remaining replay-reader gaps. Two
Kaleidoscope claims have the same public label and no visible card preview

the older reader applied Heal before the empty pick. One Armaments play
upgraded the sole eligible card directly on the native host. The opening

denominator. These are observed limits of archive admission on the frozen
host.

Both clears were unseen by the earlier three-world archive fit. On their
