## Why the runs capped

A delivered Skip closes a card preview and returns to the reward screen.
The original offer remains visible, and the converter marks that exact
offer as declined. The boss teacher's reward rule could reopen it while
trying to claim cards before leaving. Its inherited policy could also
propose the reopened offer. Repeatedly opening and declining the same
offer left the floor and deck unchanged.

The saved audit verified all eight seals and counted 6,786 delivered
reopens in the seven capped runs. The repair excludes the declined offer
from both proposal paths. A distinct card with the same label, other
rewards and the native continuation remain eligible. The versioned
runner also applies the engine's existing repeated-public-state guard.
A guard stop stays incomplete and preserves its evidence.
